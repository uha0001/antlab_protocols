# CTAB DNA Extraction for Small Ants
### by Anja Manigk

## Purpose

Extraction of genomic DNA from small ant specimens using CTAB lysis, phenol-chloroform-isoamyl alcohol extraction, isopropanol precipitation, ethanol washing, and resuspension in low-salt TE buffer.

## Materials

### Solutions

- Nuclease-free water
- Liquid nitrogen
- CTAB buffer, pre-warmed to **60°C**
- Proteinase K (**10 mg/mL**)
- DNase-free RNase (**100 mg/mL**, as listed in the source protocol)
- Phenol-chloroform-isoamyl alcohol (**25:24:1**)
- Isopropanol, ice-cold
- Glycogen (**20 mg/mL**)
- 70% ethanol, ice-cold
- Low-salt TE buffer

### Tools

- Pipette tips
- Paper towels
- 1.5 mL tubes / low-retention or low-binding tubes
- Camping stove or flame source for forceps sterilization
- Forceps
- Home-made pestles
- Eppendorf Thermomixer
- Fume hood
- Refrigerated centrifuge

---

## Day 1 — Cell Lysis

1. Label a **1.5 mL tube** and add **500 µL nuclease-free water**.
2. Clean the forceps under the flame each time before touching a sample.
3. Take an individual ant, dry it on a paper towel, and place the sample into the labelled 1.5 mL tube containing water.
4. Place samples on a shaker for **30 min at 150 rpm**.
   - While waiting, pre-warm CTAB buffer to **60°C**.
   - Prepare liquid nitrogen.
5. Label a low-binding tube with the sample ID.
6. Clean forceps under the flame and transfer the sample to the new low-binding tube.
   - Dry the sample on a paper towel before transfer.
7. Cool the pestle in liquid nitrogen and hold the sample tube in liquid nitrogen.
   - Keep the tube lid outside the liquid nitrogen because the lid may become brittle and break.
8. Pour the remaining liquid nitrogen from the pestle container into the sample tube and crush the ant using the cold home-made pestle.
9. Add **730 µL pre-warmed CTAB buffer** to each sample.
   - Use the liquid to rinse ant material from the pestle back into the tube.
10. Add **20 µL Proteinase K (10 mg/mL)**.
11. Place the tube in an Eppendorf Thermomixer and incubate **overnight at 56°C and 500 rpm**.

---

## Day 2 — Protein/RNA Removal and DNA Precipitation

12. Remove the samples from the Thermomixer.
13. Add **1.4 µL DNase-free RNase (100 mg/mL)** and incubate at **36°C for 30 min**.

### Phenol-Chloroform Extraction

Perform the following steps in a **fume hood**.

14. Add **750 µL phenol-chloroform-isoamyl alcohol (25:24:1)**.
    - The source protocol notes the reagent at **4°C**.
    - Invert and vortex directly for **3–5 s**, until the solution becomes milky.
15. Incubate on ice for **10 min**.
16. Centrifuge at **7000 rpm for 15 min at 4°C**.
    - Label a new low-binding tube while waiting.
17. Transfer the **upper phase** into the labelled 1.5 mL low-binding tube.
18. Add an **equivalent volume of ice-cold isopropanol** to the recovered upper phase (approximately **650 µL** in the source protocol).
19. Add **1 µL glycogen (20 mg/mL)**.
20. Vortex for **3–5 s**.
21. Precipitate the DNA **overnight at −20°C**.

---

## Day 3 — Purification and Resuspension

22. Centrifuge at **30,000 × g for 20 min at 4°C**.
23. Transfer the supernatant to a new 1.5 mL tube while ensuring that the formed pellet remains undisturbed.
    - The source protocol suggests keeping the supernatant at **−20°C** temporarily in case recovery is needed.
24. Add approximately **2× the pellet/sample volume** of either:
    - isopropanol, or
    - **70% ice-cold ethanol**

    The source protocol gives approximately **1.3 mL**.
25. Vortex for **3–5 s**.
26. Centrifuge at **30,000 × g for 20 min at 4°C**.
27. Remove as much supernatant as possible using a pipette.
28. Dry the pellet at **36°C on a heat block** until no ethanol remains.
29. Resuspend the pellet in **25 µL low-salt TE buffer**.
30. Shake at room temperature for **30 min at 150 rpm** before measuring DNA concentration.
31. Measure DNA concentration using a **Qubit Broad Range Kit** or other appropriate method.

## Quality Control

Record:

- sample ID
- specimen/species
- final elution volume
- DNA concentration
- total DNA yield
- any visible pellet loss or contamination
- any deviations from the protocol

## Safety Notes

- Handle **liquid nitrogen** with appropriate cryogenic PPE.
- Perform all work with **phenol-chloroform-isoamyl alcohol in a certified fume hood**.
- Dispose of organic solvent waste according to institutional procedures.
- Use caution when sterilizing forceps with an open flame.
