# Bhatkar Agar

## Ingredients

- 100 mL water
- 100 mL honey
- 1 egg
- 2 g agarose

## Approximate composition

- Agarose: ~1% (w/v), calculated from the 200 mL water + honey base

## Preparation

1. Add 100 mL water and 100 mL honey to a pot.
2. Add 2 g agarose.
3. Heat while stirring until the agarose is completely dissolved.
4. Beat 1 egg separately until homogeneous.
5. Add the egg to the agar mixture while mixing.
6. Mix thoroughly and reduce the heat.
7. Allow the mixture to cool and solidify.
